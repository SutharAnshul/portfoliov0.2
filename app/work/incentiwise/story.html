What shipped
Rule
AdminGOVERNS
Department leadDIVIDES

1
Employees can’t give

So nobody can pass points back and forth.

2
Every allowance has an owner

The admin sets it, the department lead splits it, the team lead spends it.

3
Every allowance has a ceiling

A team lead can only give what they were given this quarter.

Question

Who can spend company-funded points?

Constraint

Every point is prepaid money. Every budget needs an owner.

Decision

Only allowance holders give. Employees receive, collect and redeem.

Consequence

No send box for employees. Every point given traces back to a budget someone approved.

What that does to a team lead
Two balances.
Never mixed.
To give · company allowance
4,349
To keep · personal points
803

A lead gives recognition and receives it too. If the two numbers ever merged, a lead could spend their own reward points on the team, or quietly keep the company’s. So they live in different places and never touch.

The question underneath all of it

point worth?

Every screen that moves points has to answer it. It sounds like one question. It’s three.

01 · Face value
What does a point buy?

The store already implied it: the ₹500 Amazon card costs 500 points.

→ 1 = ₹1, fixed
02 · Funding
When does money move?

The company pays into a vault up front. Allocating points moves money that has already been paid.

→ Prepaid vault. Unused points return.
03 · Visibility
Who sees rupees?

If a thank-you shows a price, it stops feeling like a thank-you. Whoever funds it needs to see the price.
